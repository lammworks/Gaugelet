# Gaugelet brand policy

## Scope

The MIT License in [LICENSE](LICENSE) applies to Gaugelet's source code. It does not grant rights to use the Gaugelet product identity.

Any trademark rights in the **Gaugelet** name, the Gaugelet app icon, logo, wordmark, visual identity, and other files identified as brand assets are reserved by their respective owner or owners. Those assets are not licensed under MIT unless a file carries a separate explicit license.

The intended publisher brand is **LammWorks**. Before a public release identifies a legal publisher or owner, the release owner must verify the entity name, authority to publish, and corresponding Apple Developer identity. This document does not itself establish that legal ownership.

## Uses that do not require special permission

You may:

- Refer to Gaugelet accurately in articles, reviews, compatibility lists, issue reports, and discussions.
- Link to the canonical Gaugelet repository or release page.
- Use unmodified screenshots to explain or review Gaugelet, provided the context does not imply endorsement.
- State that a project is derived from Gaugelet when the derivative has a clearly different name, icon, and product identity.

## Uses that require permission

Do not, without written permission from the verified rights holder:

- Publish a fork or modified binary under the Gaugelet name.
- Reuse the Gaugelet app icon, logo, or confusingly similar artwork as another product's identity.
- Register a company, product, repository organization, domain, social account, or app listing that suggests it is the official Gaugelet publisher.
- Imply sponsorship, endorsement, certification, or partnership.
- Use Gaugelet brand assets in merchandise or paid promotional material.

## Forks and redistributions

MIT permits redistribution of the code. Public redistributions should:

1. Adopt a distinct product name and bundle identifier.
2. Replace Gaugelet icons, logos, and product-facing brand copy.
3. Retain the MIT copyright and permission notice.
4. Describe the relationship as a fork or derivative, not as an official Gaugelet release.
5. Preserve applicable third-party notices.

## OpenAI references

OpenAI, ChatGPT, Codex, and GPT are names or marks of OpenAI. Gaugelet uses ChatGPT and Codex only to describe compatibility. Do not incorporate those names, OpenAI logos, or model names into a Gaugelet fork's own product identity or imply that Gaugelet is an OpenAI product.

Gaugelet is independent software and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by OpenAI.
