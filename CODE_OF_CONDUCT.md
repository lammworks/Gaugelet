# Gaugelet Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Gaugelet welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that support an open, respectful, healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback without personal attacks.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing when appropriate, and learning from mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, threats, or intimidation.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in Gaugelet repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, reviews, and other official project spaces. It also applies when someone publicly represents Gaugelet or LammWorks in a project capacity.

## Reporting

For conduct visible on GitHub, use GitHub's built-in report controls on the relevant content or the [GitHub Report Abuse form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Maintainers may also be contacted through a verified contact method if one is later published in the repository or organization profile.

Do not publish sensitive conduct details in a Gaugelet issue. [GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/lammworks/Gaugelet/security/advisories/new) is reserved for reports that also involve a security or privacy vulnerability; it is not a general conduct mailbox. Gaugelet does not claim an unverified private email address.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. They may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this Code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Maintainers will seek to protect a reporter's privacy, assess context and impact, avoid conflicts of interest, and communicate decisions to affected participants when practical.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will use the following framework, adapted to the circumstances:

1. **Correction** — Private or public clarification for inappropriate language or behavior with no lasting community impact.
2. **Warning** — A formal warning for a single incident or series of actions, with clear conditions for continued participation.
3. **Temporary restriction** — A time-limited restriction on interaction or participation for sustained or serious violations.
4. **Permanent restriction** — Removal from project participation for repeated violations, harassment, threats, or conduct that makes continued participation unsafe.
